Why do astronauts float in space?

Astronauts float because they are in free fall! Their spacecraft orbits Earth by constantly falling toward it while also moving sideways fast enough to keep missing it. This weightless feeling is called microgravity.

✨ Fun Facts

Astronauts grow up to 5 cm taller in space because the spine stretches without gravity.
Water floats as blobs in space — astronauts drink from sealed bags!
